This is the converted Virtue and Flaw list for [Fate of Ars Magica.md](./Fate%20of%20Ars%20Magica.md), for use in **Step 5: Virtues and Flaws** of character creation. Names and concepts follow Ars Magica 5th Edition; the point costs and mechanical effects are rewritten for Fate.

## How Point Costs Work

This uses the same points from Step 5 of character creation:

- **Major** Virtue or Flaw = **3 points**
- **Minor** Virtue or Flaw = **1 point**
- A **Flaw** grants points back to spend on Virtues, or converts to a starting **fate point** if left unspent.
- A **Virtue** costs points, drawn from the character's 3-point budget (or from Flaws taken to offset it).
- Mechanically, most Virtues/Flaws below translate to one of: a **skill/sub-skill bonus**, a **stunt**, an **aspect** (with its usual free invoke / compel), or a **fate point cost/gain**.

## General Virtues and Flaws

| Name | Type | Fate Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Great/Improved/Wealthy** | Virtue (Minor) | +1 to Resources |
| **Poor/Impoverished** | Flaw (Minor/Major) | −1 (Minor) or −2 (Major) to Resources |
| **Warrior** | Virtue (Minor) | Free stunt for Fight, usable only in the described specialty |
| **Tough** | Virtue (Minor) | +1 physical stress box |
| **Puissant (Skill)** | Virtue (Minor) | One skill treated as one step higher for the purpose of choosing sub-skills, chosen skill only |
| **Affinity with (Skill)** | Virtue (Minor) | Halve the XP/study cost when raising one chosen skill |
| **Deficient (Skill)** | Flaw (Minor) | Double the XP/study cost when raising one chosen skill |
| **Educated** | Virtue (Minor) | Start with an extra Average-rated academic skill (Latin, Artes Liberales, etc. as sub-skills of an academic skill) |
| **Unaging** | Virtue (Major) | Character does not need a longevity potion to avoid natural aging |
| **Ways of the Land/Book/etc.** | Virtue (Minor) | Aspect representing a cultural specialty; free invoke once per session in the right context |
| **Difficult Underling / Weakness** | Flaw (Minor/Major) | Aspect that can be compelled for a fate point |
| **Compulsion** | Flaw (Minor/Major) | Aspect representing an addiction or obsession; compels as normal, and refusing the compulsion costs an extra fatigue level |
| **Optimist / Driven / Proud** | Virtue or Flaw (Minor) | Standard Fate aspect; free invoke or compel as appropriate |

## Social Status

Pick **one** — this sets the character's baseline place in Mythic Europe's society, same as choosing a High Concept aspect flavor.

| Name | Type | Fate Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gifted** | (all magi have this, free) | Required for Hermetic Magic; also the source of the Gift's social penalty below |
| **Wizard's Communication / Well-Traveled / Privileged Upbringing** | Virtue (Minor) | +1 to a relevant Influence or Lore sub-skill |
| **Social Contacts** | Virtue (Minor) | One extra Contacts sub-skill, free |
| **Outlaw / Blackmailed / Landless Noble** | Flaw (Minor) | Aspect representing a compromised social position; compels as normal |
| **The Gift (social penalty)** | Flaw (Major, automatic for all magi) | Aspect: *"People react to me with unease"* — compel freely in social scenes with non-magi |

## Hermetic Virtues and Flaws

For magi only — these govern how a character's magic works, layered on top of the Arts and House Stunt rules already in the core rules.

| Name | Type | Fate Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Flawless Magic** | Virtue (Major) | Formulaic spells never cost a fatigue level, even on a partial success |
| **Life-Linked Spontaneous Magic** | Virtue (Major) | Spontaneous casting can spend Confidence/fate points instead of fatigue |
| **Major/Minor Magical Focus** | Virtue (Major/Minor) | +2 (Major) or +1 (Minor) to a narrow, specific use of an Art combination (same shape as the House Stunts) |
| **Elemental Magic / Ceremonial Magic** | Virtue (Minor) | Bonus to ritual and lab-based casting; no effect on quick combat casting |
| **Deficient Technique/Form** | Flaw (Major) | −1 to any casting total that includes the chosen Art |
| **Blatant Gift** | Flaw (Minor) | As The Gift above, but the compel applies even to other magi |
| **Necromancer / Restriction** | Flaw (Minor/Major) | Aspect describing a specific limit on how the character may cast (e.g. only at night, only touching the target) |
| **Study Requirement / Special Circumstances** | Flaw (Minor) | A named condition (a familiar's advice, a specific tool, etc.) must be met before a Season of study counts toward Art advancement |

## Supernatural Virtues and Flaws

For non-Hermetic powers (Faerie, Infernal, Divine origin) — mostly for companions, or magi with an unusual origin.

| Name | Type | Fate Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Faerie/Divine/Infernal Blood** | Virtue or Flaw (Minor/Major) | Aspect reflecting the supernatural heritage; grants one themed stunt, but is freely compellable by the relevant Realm |
| **Second Sight** | Virtue (Minor) | Free sub-skill in Alertness or Investigate for detecting supernatural auras |
| **Susceptibility to (Realm)** | Flaw (Minor/Major) | −1 (Minor) or −2 (Major) to resist effects from the named Realm |
| **True Faith** | Virtue (Major) | Grants a Faith-based stunt usable against the Infernal, at the cost of following a strict code (aspect, compellable) |

## Story Flaws (GM-Facing)

These don't have a fixed Fate mechanic — they're plot hooks the Storyteller uses to bring the character into stories, worth points same as any Flaw.

- **Enemies**, **Dark Secret**, **Vow**, **Wanted by an Authority**, **Overconfident**
- Mechanically: write these as an **aspect**, and let the Storyteller compel them for fate points exactly like any other Flaw-derived aspect.
